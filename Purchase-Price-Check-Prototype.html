PRD-backed prototype

Product contract

What this prototype commits to

  • Policy determines bidding and benchmark applicability independently.
  • Comparable specification is confirmed before external contact.
  • One private round supports two to five vendors with sealed responses.
  • Source facts remain distinct from extracted and derived values.
  • Market evidence is never a bid, vendor or award candidate.
  • Unknowns, confidence, provenance and freshness stay visible.
  • A person authorizes every send, exception, award and commitment.
  • The award continues into PO and invoice variance without re-entry.

Requirement coverage

Define covers FR-001–002; Invite and Collect cover FR-003–007 and FR-012; Compare covers FR-008–009, FR-011 and FR-014; Continue covers FR-010, FR-013 and the labeled outcome contract in FR-015.

Phase 1 boundary

This is a lightweight operational purchase check for standardized goods. It is not public supplier discovery, a reverse auction, multi-round sourcing, split awards, contract management or autonomous procurement.
